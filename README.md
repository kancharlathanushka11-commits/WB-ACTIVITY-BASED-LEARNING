## Project Overview
This presentation contains website design case studies for three different themes: an electronics store, an online food ordering service, and an adventure and trekking website.

## Case 7: Electronics Store Website
Develop an online electronics catalog featuring:
- Laptops, smartphones, accessories, and smart devices
- Product comparison tables and specifications
- Discount labels and category filters
- Product detail layouts

## Case 8: Online Food Ordering Website
Design a food ordering interface featuring:
- Restaurant details and menu categories
- Food images and prices
- Special offers and customer reviews
- Order buttons
- Card layouts, attractive backgrounds, and mobile-friendly navigation

## Case 19: Adventure and Trekking Website
Create a website showcasing:
- Trekking routes and camping trips
- Difficulty levels and trip schedules
- Equipment checklists and safety guidelines
- Package prices
- Image overlays and visually distinct information panels
